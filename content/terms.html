About woolmer

woolmer.info is an independent local guide to Whitehill & Bordon and nearby villages, run by Anthony Dunn, Bordon, Hampshire (info@woolmer.info). By using the site you agree to these terms.

Listings

  • Basic listings are free. Business details start from public Food Standards Agency data and may be out of date, so check with the business before you rely on them
  • Hygiene ratings are the FSA's, not ours. Always check the current rating on the FSA website
  • Featured listings are paid placements and are always labelled. Paying doesn't affect reviews, ratings or moderation
  • We can edit, hide or remove any listing, for example if it's wrong, closed or breaks these terms

Claiming a listing

Only claim a business you're authorised to represent. We check claims before approving them. Owners are responsible for the details they add, which must be accurate and lawful. We can remove owner access if it's misused.

Accounts and posting

  • Everything you post must follow the review and Q&A guidelines
  • You keep ownership of what you post, but you give us a free, worldwide licence to show it on woolmer and to edit it for length or to remove anything that breaks the guidelines
  • Posts are checked before they go live. We can refuse or remove any post, and suspend accounts that break the rules
  • Reviews are the opinions of the people who wrote them, not woolmer's

Reporting and complaints

Use the "Report" link on any listing or post, or email info@woolmer.info. We aim to look at reports within two working days and will take down anything that's unlawful or breaks the guidelines. If a business believes a review is defamatory, email us with the details and we'll review it promptly.

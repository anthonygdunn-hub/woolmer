Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Who we are

woolmer (woolmer.info) is an independent guide to Whitehill & Bordon, run by Anthony Dunn in Bordon, Hampshire. You can contact us at info@woolmer.info.

What we collect, and why

Before launch, the only personal information we collect is what you type into the sign-up form:

  • your email address, and your name if you give it, so we can tell you when woolmer launches
  • if you run a business: the business name, type, a phone number if you give one and any message, so we can prepare your free listing and contact you about it

We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Where it's kept

Sign-ups are stored in a database run by Supabase, on servers in the EU. Only we can read them. We don't sell your details, share them with advertisers or use them for anything other than the reasons above.

Business information

Some business details on woolmer, such as names, addresses and food hygiene ratings, come from the Food Standards Agency's public food hygiene rating data. We don't publish details of home-based or mobile food businesses unless the owner asks us to.

Cookies

This page doesn't use cookies or tracking. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How long we keep it

We keep sign-ups until woolmer launches and for up to 12 months after, unless you become a listed business or ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to us using it, by emailing info@woolmer.info. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

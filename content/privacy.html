Who we are

woolmer (woolmer.info) is an independent guide to Whitehill & Bordon, run by Anthony Dunn in Bordon, Hampshire. We're the data controller for the information described here. Contact us at info@woolmer.info.

What we collect, and why

  • Sign-up form: your email, your name if you give it, and business details if you run one. Used to tell you about woolmer and prepare your listing. Lawful basis: consent
  • Your account: your email address (to send sign-in links) and the display name you choose. Lawful basis: providing the service you asked for
  • Reviews, questions and answers: what you write, your display name, your rating and when you posted. Shown publicly once checked. Your email is never shown
  • Listing claims: your name, role, phone number and any evidence you give us, used only to check you're allowed to manage the listing
  • Reports: the reason you give when you report something, so we can deal with it

Business information

Business names, addresses and food hygiene ratings come from the Food Standards Agency's open data, used under the Open Government Licence. We don't publish home-based or mobile food businesses unless the owner asks us to. Owners can claim, correct or ask us to remove a listing by emailing us.

Where it's kept and who sees it

Data is stored by Supabase on servers in the EU (Ireland). Sign-in emails are sent through our email provider. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't sell your details, and we don't share them with advertisers.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. If you sign in, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay signed in; that's essential for the account to work. Maps use OpenStreetMap tiles, fonts come from Google Fonts and one script library comes from jsDelivr; those services see your IP address when your browser loads them.

How long we keep it

  • Accounts, reviews and answers: until you delete them or ask us to
  • Rejected or removed posts and reports: up to 12 months, so we can deal with complaints
  • Claims: for as long as you manage the listing, plus 12 months
  • Sign-ups that don't become accounts or listings: up to 12 months after launch

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@woolmer.info. We'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

You need to be 16 or over to create an account.

woolmer only works if people can trust what they read. Every review, question and answer is checked by a person before it goes live.

Do

  • Write about your own, recent experience
  • Be specific: what you had, what was good, what wasn't
  • Be fair, even when you're disappointed
  • Keep questions useful to other locals

Don't

  • Review a business you own, work for, are related to or compete with
  • Post a review you were paid, offered a discount or asked to write in return for something
  • Name or describe individual staff in a negative way, or share anyone's personal details
  • Make claims you can't back up, such as accusing a business of a crime or of making people ill. Report health concerns to East Hampshire District Council's environmental health team instead
  • Post links, adverts, spam, hate, threats, swearing at people or anything unlawful
  • Post more than one review of the same business

Business owners

  • You can reply once, publicly and politely, to each review of your business
  • You can't remove a review because it's negative. If you think one breaks these guidelines, use "Report this review"
  • Don't offer customers anything in return for reviews

What we do

We approve posts that follow these guidelines, reject ones that don't, and may edit a post only to remove something that breaks them. Repeated or serious breaches mean your account is suspended. Questions about a decision: info@woolmer.info.
